New Feature · Live Aug 13

Download an Individual Employee’s Current-Year Draft W-2

Employers can now preview and download one employee’s current-year draft W-2 directly from the W-2s and 1099s page.

🔒 INTERNAL ONLY — NO EXTERNAL SHARING ALLOWED. Do not download, forward, or show this recording to customers.
▶ Watch the internal walkthrough in SlackHomebase sign-in required · Recording stays behind Slack authentication
📄
What sales needs to know: On Team → W-2s and 1099s, select the current year to see employees with a Draft badge. Clicking a file link generates that employee’s draft W-2 PDF in the browser.
🧭
Why it changed: Employers previously had no in-product way to retrieve an individual employee’s current-year draft. The existing bulk draft ZIP is a separate year-end flow available November 15–December 31.
💬
How it impacts your day-to-day: Direct customers to Team → W-2s and 1099s, choose 2026, then click the employee’s PDF. Reinforce that it is a draft—not a filed form—and amounts can change after later payrolls. Draft 1099 previews are not included.
Who sees it: About 13,500 companies have at least one paid 2026 payroll run and are most likely to see draft rows today. Review the release details in Slack.
Feature Improvement · Live Aug 12

Autopayroll Reminders Now Flag Timecards Needing Review

The upcoming-submission email gives customers the timecard details they need to resolve before Autopayroll runs.

The reminder summarizes open issues and links directly to timecard review.
⏰
What sales needs to know: The reminder now includes pay period, payday, automatic-submission time, a count of timecards needing review, and a breakdown of missing clock-outs, missing breaks, no-shows, and unscheduled shifts.
✅
Why it changed: Customers asked for enough warning to resolve timecard issues before payroll submits. When nothing is open, the email confirms that no timecards currently need review.
👉
How it impacts your day-to-day: If a customer asks about the reminder, point them to the Review timecards button and Timesheets pay-period review. The email does not include an estimated bank-debit amount.
Available to: 1,338 Autopayroll-enabled companies see the new content on their next reminder. Read the release post in Slack.
Staged Rollout · Began Aug 11

Roles & Wages Redesign Starts with New Companies

The first phase gives a small group of newly created companies a clearer, effective-dated way to manage roles and wages.

▶ Watch the Roles & Wages walkthrough
💵
What sales needs to know: About 1% of newly created companies now see Roles & Wages under Team → team member → Job Details. They can add or remove roles, change wages, choose a primary role, switch hourly or salaried status, and apply wage changes across selected locations.
🗓️
Why it changed: Homebase is rebuilding wage history so changes are stored accurately. The new experience handles one intentional action at a time and asks when it should take effect.
🧩
How it impacts your day-to-day: Most customers—including existing companies—still have the prior editor. If a new customer sees the redesign, guide them through the action and effective date. Warn that switching an hourly employee to salaried removes additional hourly roles.
Expectation setting: This is the first controlled phase, not a broad migration. Review the release details in Slack.
📣 PMM
Heads-Up · Targeting Sept 2

Payroll Base Price Is Planned to Increase from $39 to $49

The Payroll base price point for impacted existing customers is planned to move from $39 to $49, with Marketing targeting September 2 for the customer announcement.

📬
What sales needs to know: The Payroll base price point for impacted existing customers is planned to increase from $39 to $49. Marketing is targeting September 2 to email every impacted current Payroll customer, provided there are no planning or leadership delays.
🤝
Why this heads-up matters: CX and Sales need one coordinated response before customers receive the announcement, especially when pricing questions raise churn risk.
🗣️
How it impacts your day-to-day: Watch for a combined CX and Sales rollout in the next one to two weeks with the approved talk tracks. Use the confirmed $39-to-$49 base-price change, but do not speculate about other pricing, eligibility, or retention details before the guidance arrives.
Timing is not final: Treat September 2 as the target—not a promise—until the enablement rollout confirms the audience, message, and response plan.
