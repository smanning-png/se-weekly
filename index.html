Sales Connect Widgets — Launching Week of June 8

Payroll self-serve (SS) flow pages are getting sales-team CTAs like “Call me now” during business hours and “Schedule a call” outside business hours. “X rep is calling you” alerts are also coming into the in-app customer experience.

Payroll SS setup page — Sales Connect CTA entry point
Incoming-call state shown after a specialist starts calling
▶ Watch Loom walkthrough — CTCB Within Business Hours
📣
What sales needs to know: Sales Connect is launching the week of June 8. Payroll SS flow pages will surface CTAs like “Call me now” and “Schedule a call” so customers can reach Sales while setting up payroll. Rollout/launch training for the team is Tues, June 9.
🧩
Why it’s changing: Payroll setup is a high-intent, high-friction moment. The team is putting clearer sales hand-raises directly into that flow and adding “X rep is calling you” alerts so customers know when a rep is actively calling.
📞
How it impacts day-to-day: Expect more context-rich inbound requests from Payroll setup. Connect the customer’s current setup step to the next best action, whether that is answering a question live, booking follow-up, or moving them toward implementation.
⚠️
Scope guardrail: The “X rep is calling you” alert is part of the in-app customer experience and is intentionally focused on Payroll setup moments where the customer has already shown intent.
Main callout: Customer CTAs — like “Call me now” during business hours or “Schedule a call” outside business hours — are coming to Payroll SS flow pages so customers can reach Sales at the point they need help. “X rep is calling you” alerts are also coming to the in-app customer experience.
Visual Refresh

Designbase Theme Refresh — Updated App Visual System

Designbase is refreshing core visual styles across web and mobile using shared tokens for colors, spacing, sizing, strokes, and common components. There are no workflow or feature changes.

Updated duotone icon styling for web and mobile surfaces
🎨
What sales needs to know: Customers may notice subtle visual differences as updated colors, spacing, strokes, and component styles roll through the app.
🧱
Why it’s changing: Web and mobile styles had drifted over time. The refresh creates a shared token-based foundation so Homebase looks more cohesive and stays consistent as new features ship.
💬
How it impacts day-to-day: If a customer says something looks different, confirm it’s expected and non-disruptive. Navigation, workflows, and product behavior are unchanged.
🐞
Escalation rule: Do not escalate normal cosmetic differences. File a bug only if something is actually broken: overlapping text, cut-off content, unreadable colors, or a blocked workflow.
Customer-ready wording: “You may notice small visual updates as Homebase refreshes the app experience. Functionality is the same; it’s a design-system cleanup to make the product feel more consistent.”

Hiring V1 → V2 Migration Reminder — Small Notice

LCM is sending a reminder email on June 3 to customers still using the V1 Hiring tool, nudging them toward the updated Hiring Assistant experience.

📬
What sales needs to know: Customers still on Hiring V1 may receive a migration reminder email on June 3.
🎯
Why it’s changing: The migration push is continuing through lifecycle comms, with the goal of having no remaining V1 Hiring users by July 14.
💬
How it impacts day-to-day: Expect light follow-up questions from V1 customers. Reassure them that V2/Hiring Assistant is the intended path and route implementation-specific concerns through the existing Hiring migration guidance.
Keep it light: This is a customer-communication notice, not a new product launch. Use it to explain why a Hiring customer may be asking about migration this week.
