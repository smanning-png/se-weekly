Process Update · Live

Blitz Beta Cadences

The Blitz outreach experience has been rebuilt from a same-day call-heavy motion into two auto-routed 5-day cadences. Reps see the new experience automatically when switching into Blitz mode.

1
Day 1
Manual calls plus auto SMS.
2
Day 2
Auto SMS plus automated value-prop email using the assigned rep's calendar link and signature.
3
Days 3–4
Manual follow-up calls keep the motion active without requiring reps to draft every touch manually.
4
Day 5
Breakup SMS closes the loop.
📌
What sales needs to know: Reps do not choose the cadence manually — accounts are routed automatically from existing customer data.
🧠
Why it's happening: Blitz is being made more consistent and easier to execute, with less doc-switching and more guided outreach.
☎️
How it impacts your day-to-day: Use the right-panel call scripts for live answers, response branches, bad timing, and voicemail guidance. No more jumping between docs during outreach.
🛡️
Carryover protections: Previously disqualified accounts stay disqualified, and cooldown logic carries over to avoid over-touching recently worked accounts.
Small but useful UI improvement: Past opps now show Product Family in Pipeline and Blitz views, directly beneath loss reason, so reps get faster historical context before reaching out.
