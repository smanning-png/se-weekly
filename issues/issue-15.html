Homebase Boost + General Application Roles

Homebase Boost UI examples — compressed internal visual from supplied screenshots

Beta Expansion

Homebase Assistant Alpha Expands to ~1K Beta Users

Homebase Assistant Alpha has moved into a broader beta audience, with web launch email and discovery nudge live. Mobile engagement is still blocked by Amplitude SDK configuration for deep links.

📌
What sales needs to know: Homebase Assistant Alpha is now in front of roughly 1K beta users. The launch email saw a 52% open rate, and the web discovery nudge has 304 views and 19 completions.
🧪
Why it’s happening / changing: PMM and LCM are testing whether owners understand and engage with Assistant beyond starter questions before treating it as a broader GTM story.
💬
How it impacts day-to-day: Keep this as awareness, not a promise. If prospects ask about AI assistance, say testing is expanding and the team is learning what owners actually use before wider positioning.
⚠️
Watchout: Mobile nudges are still pending Amplitude SDK/deep-link configuration, so do not position this as fully cross-platform yet.
Field stance: Treat Assistant as a signal of direction, not a packaged sales claim. Capture questions and feedback, but do not overpromise availability or scope.
GTM Planning

What’s Shaping Q3 Targeting

The Q1 direct-mail 60-day read is giving PMM early targeting signals for Q3 planning: employee size is the clearest lever, and Caregiving plus Medical/Vet are the strongest industry signals.

📌
What sales needs to know: Early Q1 direct-mail analysis shows no clean winner between letter vs. postcard yet, but the audience signals are clearer than the format signals.
📈
Why it’s happening / changing: At 60 days, employee size is showing the clearest conversion lift, with paid conversion jumping materially for larger employee bands. Caregiving and Medical/Vet are the strongest industry standouts.
🎯
How it impacts day-to-day: Expect Q3 campaigns and list builds to lean harder into higher-employee-count businesses and select verticals. Use the signal as context, not as a rep-level targeting rule until PMM publishes approved plays.
PMM caveat: These are 60-day reads; the standard matchback window is 90 days. Treat this as directional Q3 planning input, not a finished GTM decision.
