Promo Update · Existing Customers

Spring Payroll Promo — Wave 2 Hits Existing Customers May 19

The Wave 2 push of the Spring Payroll Promo extends 3 months free on Payroll to existing customers. The offer is auto-applied for the vast majority of cases — but there's a narrow exception window (May 11–19) where sales needs to add the package manually. Watch the walkthrough below for examples.

▶ Watch: Spring Payroll Promo — exception cases walkthrough (4:55) · by Pablo Tac
📅
May 19 — Wave 2 launches: In-app banners and email campaign extend the promo to existing customers. Wave 1 to net new went live May 11. Campaign runs through July 15, 2026.
💬
Different conversation than Wave 1: Wave 1 (new customers) was an acquisition push. Wave 2 is broader and softer — lead with the offer, but anchor on retention and ongoing feature value. These customers already know Homebase.
🏷️
Competitive context: Aggressive off-season payroll discounting is now industry standard. Gusto, QuickBooks, ADP, Patriot, and Sure Payroll are all running promos. Our 3 months free is positioned to match.

🛠️ Manual package logic — how to spot exception cases

1
Package name: 39/6 — 3 months free
If you need to add the promo to an existing customer in Admin manually, this is the package to apply. After the free period, standard pricing is $39/mo + $6/employee.
2
When manual adds are allowed: May 11–19 only
During the gap week between Wave 1 (new customers, May 11) and Wave 2 (existing customers, May 19), sales should only manually add the package for exception-based existing customers. After May 19, the system auto-applies — no manual add needed.
3
Salesforce banners tell you where the customer fits
Look for these tags on the opportunity in SF:
  • "New Customer — 3 mos free promo" — net-new lead, eligible since May 11. System handles the offer.
  • "Existing Customer — 3 mos free promo" — eligible since May 19. Auto-applied from in-app + email.
4
Eligibility checklist
Net-new signups during the promo window OR existing customers without Payroll. Current Payroll customers do not qualify. Add-on works with any plan tier (Essentials, Plus, All-in-One).
5
The hard deadline: July 15, 2026
To qualify, the customer must sign up during the promo window AND run their first payroll by July 15. If payroll runs after that, standard pricing kicks in with no free period. Use this for urgency in late-cycle conversations.
Treat any existing-customer payroll conversation this week as promo-eligible. The offer auto-applies from May 19, so you don't need to dig into Admin in most cases. If you do hit an exception (existing customer not seeing the offer between May 11–19), use package 39/6 manually. The Loom walkthrough above shows the most common cases.

"Clocked In" Monthly Release Email — Launches May 19

A new monthly product update email goes to all active Homebase customers starting May 19. Two sections: Quality & Reliability and What's New. The goal is to build trust by giving customers a regular, transparent window into recent product improvements and bug fixes.

Preview: the first edition of "Clocked In" launching to all active customers May 19.
1
Section 1 — Quality & Reliability
Bug fixes, performance improvements, and reliability wins shipped in the past 30 days. Designed to surface the unsung work that makes Homebase easier to use.
2
Section 2 — What's New
Features and improvements customers can actually use. Curated, not exhaustive — the goal is recognition, not feature list overload.
3
Monthly cadence, all active customers
First send goes out Monday May 19. Will repeat every month going forward as a steady reliability signal — building trust and improving NPS over time.
🎯
Why this matters: Customers don't always see the volume of fixes and small improvements rolling out behind the scenes. A consistent monthly summary closes that perception gap and builds trust through transparency.
💬
SE talking point: Reference Clocked In when customers raise reliability concerns or bring up past bugs. "You'll start seeing a monthly summary of fixes and shipped features in your inbox" is a solid retention save.
Customers will see this in their inboxes starting May 19. If a customer mentions "I never know what's happening with the product" or "is anything getting fixed?" — point them to Clocked In. The rhythm itself is the message.
